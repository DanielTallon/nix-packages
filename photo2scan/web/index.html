photo2scan

Turn phone photos of paper pages into a straightened, scanned-looking PDF. PDFs and Word files (.docx) work too: each of their pages is straightened and cleaned the same way. Everything happens in this browser; your files are never uploaded.

Drop photos, PDFs or Word files here, or use Choose files….
Pages appear in the order you add them; use the arrows to reorder.
Drag the blue corners if a page's outline is off. The result updates when you let go. To sign a page, use its Sign button, then drag the signature into place on the result and drag its bottom-right corner to resize it.
